Stretch or replace

Carpet stretching vs. replacement in Bellevue

Half the "we need new carpet" calls we get in Bellevue are really a stretching job — about a fifth of the cost. We tell you which one you're looking at, even when the cheap answer is the one that pays us less.

Stretch it

Ripples and waves, pile still standing
Pulled loose at a doorway or wall
Color you still like, under 8 years old
Backing intact, pad still springy

Replace it

Matted traffic paths that won't fluff back
Pet odor that survived cleaning
Seams splitting or backing delaminated
Pad flattened to cardboard
