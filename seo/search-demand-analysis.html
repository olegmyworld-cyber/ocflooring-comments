Two things before the numbers

This is the Seattle market, not Orange County. Every query in this data is Puget Sound — Bellevue, Everett, Lynnwood, Redmond, Snohomish, Mercer Island. "OC" here reads as the company name, not the region.

I could not use your Chrome or Semrush. I run in a cloud container with no access to your machine or your logged-in Semrush session. So this is built on Search Console instead — which is better for this question anyway: Semrush estimates national volume, Search Console shows the actual queries that put your site in front of your customers.

Google shows your site nearly a million times a year and about thirteen people a day click. That is low for a site with 400 pages, but the distribution matters more than the rate: your service pages are almost invisible. All twenty of them combined earned 256 clicks — fewer than a single blog post about carpet brands.

That reframes the job. The problem is not page count. It is that your commercial pages don't rank, and the categories your customers actually search have no service page at all.

Read this before you build anything

About 66,000 of those impressions are not real people

Forty queries sit in Google's top 12 results, pull 400+ impressions each, and earn exactly zero clicks. At position 3–8, real searchers click 2–10% of the time. Zero, forty times over, is not a ranking problem — it is automated traffic.

Practical effect: ignore raw impressions as your build signal. Everything ranked below is weighted by clicks, which bots don't generate.

Build list

Six category pages, in priority order

Ranked by verified click demand against what your site currently has. I checked all ~400 indexed URLs to confirm each gap.

1
No page exists

Carpet Installation

/services/carpet-installation

Your biggest hole by a wide margin, and the page-level numbers make it decisive. Carpet content earned 798 clicks this year — 16% of your entire site — with no carpet service page in existence.

Two blog posts are doing all of it: the-10-best-carpet-brands-reviews-2023-guide at 537 clicks (your #2 page after the homepage) and what-is-a-good-price-to-pay-for-carpet-in-seattle-wa at 238 clicks on 110,158 impressions — the single most-seen page you own.

For contrast, your entire /services/ tree earned 256 clicks. Carpet, with no service page, out-earns every service page you have by more than 3×. The blogs sit at position 13.6 and 21.0 — page two — because a blog post cannot rank for a commercial query like "carpet installation seattle". Give carpet a real service hub with pricing, brands and the estimate flow, and link both blogs into it as feeders.

Targets
carpet installation cost · carpet cost per square foot · carpet installation seattle · best carpet brands · carpet replacement cost

Carpet city pages for Seattle, Bellevue, Lynnwood, Bothell, Mill Creek — the only service in your catalog with no city coverage at all.

Hardwood Floor Refinishing — one consolidated hub

/services/hardwood-floor-refinishing

This is your highest-intent service and your worst structural problem. Nine pages currently compete for the same query set: /services/floor-refinishing, /blog/hardwood-floor-refinishing, /blog/cost-to-refinish-hardwood-floors, /blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-refinish-hardwood-floors, /blog/hardwood-floor-refinishing-cost-seattle-wa, /services/dustless-floor-sanding and its -copy duplicate, /services/buff-and-recoat-hardwood-floors, plus two eco-friendly refinishing pages.

Google splits authority across all of them, so none reaches page one. Build one authoritative hub — process, dustless sanding, buff & recoat, timeline, cost — and 301 the weakest duplicates into it.

Targets
hardwood floor refinishing · floor refinishing near me · hardwood floor refinishing seattle · cost to refinish hardwood floors
Upside
These already earn 60+ clicks from positions 29–48. Page one is a multiple, not a percentage.

Stain Color Gallery & DuraSeal Chart

/stain-colors

Your best CTR of any category — 0.44%, triple the site average — and you already rank well: duraseal stain colors at position 9.3 with 34 clicks, most popular duraseal stain colors at 8.7, duraseal chestnut on white oak at 4.6.

Somebody searching a stain color has already decided to refinish. They are picking the shade. Build a proper visual gallery: every DuraSeal color shot on white oak, red oak, and maple — using photos of your own jobs, which nobody else in the market can copy — with a "book a sample viewing" CTA on each swatch.

One generic page

Floor Repair — split by material

/services/floor-repair/{hardwood|vinyl-plank|laminate}

Repair ties stain colors for your best CTR at 0.45%, and you are already winning the vinyl half: vinyl floor repair ranks 1.4 and vinyl flooring repair ranks 1.8. Meanwhile hardwood floor repair languishes at position 32 on 1,619 impressions.

One /services/flooring-repair page is trying to serve three different materials and three different customers. Split it. Repair jobs are also the cheapest way to open a door that later becomes a whole-house refinish.

Targets

Pricing Hub

/pricing

103,118 impressions on cost queries at 0.08% CTR and an average position of 31.7 — the biggest volume you rank badly for. Cost pages also convert: how much does it cost to refinish 500 square feet of hardwood floors earned 4 clicks from just 202 impressions.

You have /flooring-calculator buried at 821 impressions. Promote it into a real pricing hub: per-square-foot ranges by service, what changes the price, and the calculator front and center. Publish actual numbers — the competitors who hide pricing are why these queries are winnable.

Brand Landing Pages

/brands/{crestwood|kentwood|stonewood|reward}

crestwood flooring brought 11 clicks from position 14.8, and abode by kentwood another 4 from position 6.8. You carry roughly 150 product SKUs across Kentwood, Crestwood, Stonewood, Reward and Triangulo, but every one is an orphan product page with no brand hub above it.

Cheap to build — the products already exist. A brand page per line, collecting its SKUs with pricing and "see it in our Lynnwood showroom", captures shoppers who are researching a specific floor and don't yet know who installs it locally.

Targets

Skip these

Where I'd tell you not to spend the money

  • 20 × "hardwood floor cleaning [city] wa" pages. On raw impressions this looks like a 20,000-impression opportunity across twenty cities. It is the bot cluster from earlier — 0.02% CTR, five clicks in a year, and you already rank position 2–9 for it. Building pages will not change a number that is not coming from people.
  • Tile. 5,025 impressions, zero clicks, and heavily concentrated in the same synthetic Mercer Island pattern. If you want tile as a business line, that is a business decision — the search data does not support it on its own.
  • Stairs. 1,291 impressions and zero clicks. You already have two stair pages. Leave it.
  • More city × service pages. You have around 300 of them and they average under 3,000 impressions each. The matrix is saturated. The exception is carpet, which has none.
