Site nwocflooring.com Date 8 Sep 2026 Prepared for Oleg Chaikovskyi Platform Webflow
The website is finished. The bottleneck is reviews, not code.

The site ranks first among every local contractor on its own keywords, across King County, on desktop. Every technical item found this week is fixed and live. What stops those rankings turning into calls is the Google map pack and Yelp, which sit above the organic results and take the click. Both are decided by review count, not by anything on the website.

Next work that pays: reviews on the Business Profile after every job, and five real city pages for the cities the jobs actually come from. Everything else on this page is monitoring.

Content and geography

The site has 180 city pages across 30 cities and six services, built from one template. Pages near Bellevue rank; pages far from it don't. That is proximity and the Business Profile doing the ranking, not the page, and it is also why Google declines to index part of the set.

QuickBooks invoices for the last 12 months, matched to Calendly booking addresses, show where the refinishing work actually comes from. Only the 53% of revenue with a booking address is counted.

Do not do

  • Buy links, guest posts, or "PBN" placements of any kind. The one thing that earns a manual penalty.
  • Add more templated city pages to this site. 180 already produce under one click per page per quarter.
  • Work Semrush's 107 on-page "ideas" or chase its toxicity score. Neither moves a refinishing job.
  • Import a redirect file. Webflow's importer replaces the entire list; the 187 rules live today are correct.
  • Read weekly position tracking. Single-searcher keywords swing 20 places on noise.
