# Wills Flooring website audit — willsflooring.com

Date: 2026-09-08. Site: Webflow `66cc1f4542ee0c42e43d0303` (Wills Flooring). Domains: willsflooring.com, www.willsflooring.com.
Sources: Webflow Data API (all 152 pages, 9 CMS collections, site/page custom code), Google Search Console (Sep 2025 – Sep 7 2026), Semrush domain snapshot, Calendly bookings (Jun 8 – Sep 8 2026). The live site is blocked by the sandbox proxy, so rendering was not checked in a browser; everything below comes from the published Webflow source.

## Verdict

The site's one job is to book estimates, and the booking path is broken, hidden or mislabeled on most of its ~390 indexable URLs. Meanwhile organic clicks fell from 139/mo (Jan) to 40/mo (Aug) on flat impressions. Roughly 30 URLs are real pages; the other ~360 are cloned city pages, junk product pages and blank CMS pages that dilute the site and give Google reasons to trust it less.

Bookings still happen (Calendly, "Wills Flooring" event): Jun 16, Jul 22, Aug 13, Sep 2 so far. Those are not coming from organic search (≈1 non-branded click/day). They come from ads, referrals and direct. So the fixes below matter for paid traffic first.

**Do:** a one-day fix pass on the P0 list this week (Andrey in Webflow, or me through the Webflow API), then publish. Spend nothing on new SEO content, Kova or more city pages until P0 and P1 are done and October numbers are in.
**That flips if** the Oct 7 review shows Search Console still under 60 clicks/mo after the fixes → then the decision is to noindex the 128 city pages and the 122 blank pages, not to write more content.

The question you should have asked: **how many estimates does the site book per source?** Nobody can answer that today. There is no GA4 property for Wills, the Webflow tag field is empty, and Calendly bookings carry no UTM. Fixing measurement is item 15.
