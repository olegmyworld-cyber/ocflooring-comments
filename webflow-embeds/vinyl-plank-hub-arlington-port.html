What "mil" actually means

A mil is 1/1,000 of an inch — the thickness of the clear wear layer on top of the plank. It's the part that takes the scratches, so it's the single biggest factor in how long your floor lasts. Thicker wear layer = longer life.

  • 6–8 milBedrooms, light traffic, low-use rentals.
  • 12 milBusy family homes with kids and pets — our most-recommended.
  • 20+ milHeavy traffic, big dogs, light-commercial wear.

We match the wear layer to how your home actually lives — whether that's a Bellevue condo or a Marysville family house — and never upsell you past what you need.

Your 1–2 day install — what to expect

  1. Before we start: we confirm your subfloor is sound and let the planks acclimate to your home.
  2. Day 1: we protect and clear the rooms, prep and level the subfloor, then begin laying plank.
  3. Day 2 (if needed): finish install, cut in transitions and trim, reset baseboards, full clean-up.
  4. Same day you're done: walkthrough + a simple care guide — and you can walk on it right away.

When vinyl plank isn't the right call

We'd rather lose the sale than float a floor over a problem. We'll tell you up front if:

  • Your subfloor needs leveling or moisture sealing first.
  • A failing or very uneven slab needs repair before any install.
  • Refinishing your existing hardwood is the better long-term value for your home.

An honest "not yet" or "not this" saves you money — and it's how we earn referrals on both sides of the county line.

Subfloor prep, said plainly

Most callbacks in this trade come from skipped prep, not the plank. Before a single board goes down we check for moisture, flatness, and movement — and we'll show you what we find.

If your floor is install-ready, great. If it needs leveling or a moisture barrier, you'll get the reason and the cost in writing before we proceed — no surprises mid-job.
