Our 5-Step Hardwood Refinishing Process

We refinish hardwood the clean way — removing only a thin layer of wood and using professional dust-free systems for safe, lasting results on every project across Seattle, Bellevue & the Eastside.

  1. 1

    Prep & Protect

    We move light furniture, seal off rooms, and sheet everything in plastic for a clean, worry-free start.

  2. 2

    Dustless Sanding

    We remove old finish and scratches with top-grade dustless equipment — leaving your space 99% dust-free.

  3. 3

    Spot Repairs & Filling

    We repair nail holes, cracks, and wear for a smooth, clean surface that's ready for a flawless refinish.

  4. 4

    Stain & Finish

    Choose the look you love. We apply premium stain and durable finishes that bring lasting beauty to your floors.

  5. 5

    Final Walkthrough

    Before we leave, we walk every step with you to make sure you're 100% happy with your new floors.

Choosing your finish

We finish with Bona’s waterborne sealers — from a bright Scandinavian whitewash to a warm, oil-rubbed amber glow. Here’s how the five compare:

  • Bona NordicSealLightest — Bona's strongest white pigment for a bright, Scandinavian whitewashed look on any wood. Best for cool, modern light interiors that want a crisp, airy floor.
  • Bona NaturalSealLight — Just a hint of white pigment — keeps the raw, just-sanded unfinished wood look. Best for the natural raw look.
  • Bona ClassicSealSoft Warm — Bona's standard clear sealer with a slight, natural warming of the wood. Best for everyday warmth on natural wood.
  • Bona IntenseSealRich · Favorite — No pigment, but deepens and enriches the wood's natural color and any stain beneath it. Best for stained floors you want to look deeper and more vibrant.
  • Bona AmberSealWarmest — Amber pigment for a warm, oil-rubbed glow — the oil-modified poly look, without oil's downsides. Best for warm, traditional floors.

Actual color varies by wood species and floor condition — we always do an on-site sample for your approval before coating.
