Tile Installation Questions

How much does tile installation cost?
Most projects run $14–$26 per square foot installed, depending on tile format and prep; labor-only pricing is available from $11/sq ft. Every quote is itemized in writing at your free in-home estimate.
What is the 24-hour flood test?
Before any tile goes on a shower, we plug the drain and flood the waterproofed base for 24 hours, then photograph the result. It proves the membrane holds water — so the tile above it never has to.
Can I buy my own tile and have you install it?
Yes. Bring tile from any store — we build the waterproofing and substrate under it to the same standard, so an inexpensive tile lasts like a premium one.
Do you do heated tile floors?
Yes — electric radiant systems under bathroom, kitchen and entry tile are one of our most requested upgrades.
Is the work warrantied?
Tile installations carry a 2-year workmanship warranty on top of the photographed flood-test documentation you receive.
