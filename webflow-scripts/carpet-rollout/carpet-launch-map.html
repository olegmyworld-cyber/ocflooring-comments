After you publish

Everything is staged and out of draft. One publish takes all thirty live, plus the nav and slider changes. Then five things are worth eyeballing.

  1. Open any two city pages side by side.

    Confirm the writing reads differently — the hero paragraph and the FAQ answers should feel like separate pages, not one page with the city swapped.

  2. Check the Services menu.

    "Carpet Installation" should sit under Floor Repair in the dropdown, on every page of the site.

  3. Find the new slider category.

    In "Proudly Serving Homeowners", a Carpet Installation Service Areas slide now joins Refinishing, LVP, Hardwood and Repair. On carpet pages it shows first. Click through the arrows once to confirm the fifth slide is reachable.

  4. Send one test photo quote.

    From any city page. The email should arrive with that city's name in the subject line.

  5. Request indexing for a few.

    In Google Search Console, submit two or three of the new URLs — the rest follow from your sitemap and the links between pages.

One judgement call worth knowing: prices, warranty terms and the "20+ samples" promise read the same on every page — they have to, because they're the same offer. The repeated wording that remains is almost entirely those facts. Everything around them is city-specific.
